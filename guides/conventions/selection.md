# Conventions: the competition selection

This guide holds the narration behind the `AGENTS.md` "Conventions" bullet "The competition is decided ONCE, at the edge, and then travels as a value", moved out of `AGENTS.md` word for word. It also holds what `.cursor/rules/surface-parity.mdc` stated that no bullet does, under a heading naming the rule. Its last section holds a bullet whole, as it stood before `AGENTS.md` kept only its lead and the sentences an agent holds on every task. `AGENTS.md` stays canonical.

## The competition is decided ONCE, at the edge, and then travels as a value

Twenty-four call sites used to re-resolve it mid-request.

## From `.cursor/rules/surface-parity.mdc`

### Every answer names its competition (the mode line and the `competition` key)

A saved mode line names no source (`World Cup`, not `World Cup · …`). A new competition-answering command or tool gets the line and the key; a new emit site spreads `selectionExtras`, never a hand-built key.

### The pinned team, and the ambient pick (a preference, never a filter)

The team-taking surfaces take, in order, the argument, `CLAUDINHO_TEAM`, the pin, on every surface: CLI `next`, `share next`, `markets next` (through `teamAsked`), MCP `get_next_fixture` with `team` omitted (`nextAsked`: the server's `CLAUDINHO_TEAM`, else the pin); the pin answers as a RESOLVED team (`nextFixtureForPin`: by its id, or by code for an id-less pin, believed on the bundled competition alone; never resolved again, no roster read). As a team to answer about, on MCP the pin reaches `get_next_fixture` alone, by design: `get_share_snippet` without a team and `get_market_signal` without one stay a date's answer; as the cry's tiebreak it reaches every tool that prints a match line (`get_today`, `get_live`, `get_match`, `get_next_fixture`) through `fmtOpts`, and `fixtures://` prints a phrase, never a cry. **World Cup exception of `follow --team`:** an unknown 3-letter code is refused (a pin must be a team the roster holds), where `next` passes one through as the escape hatch for other feeds. The ambient surfaces (the statusline's live line, syncing matchup and countdown; the hook's list; `vibe`'s segment) pick through ONE function, `pickAmbientMatch(matches, pick)` (`packages/cli/src/statusline.ts`), the pick being `CLAUDINHO_TEAM`'s code, else the pin when `CLAUDINHO_TEAM` is absent (present but unreadable on the hot path, a name off the bundle, it is still the override: no preference, never the pin): the picked team's matches FIRST, the others kept (the statusline shows the picked live match and counts the rest as `+N`; the hook lists them after it). **Parity exception, stated:** this replaced the statusline's filter-to-one and `vibe`'s filter (0.11 · 2.5b): with the picked team not live, the line shows what is, and with no fixture of the picked team ahead, the countdown is to anyone's. A new ambient surface asks `pickAmbientMatch`, never a filter of its own.

## The bullet whole: "The competition is decided ONCE, at the edge, and then travels as a value"

The `AGENTS.md` "Conventions" bullet as it stood on `main` before this section was added, word for word. `AGENTS.md` now keeps its lead and a selection of its sentences and clauses, with a few joining words and a closing sentence of its own. A rule stated only here still holds: a sentence `AGENTS.md` leaves out is not a rule withdrawn. A reference in this text to "above" or to another bullet is to `AGENTS.md`: the bullets before this one there, and for one of the four bullets held whole, its own guide. One clause of it is looser than the reader: "a pin that is not exactly `{ id?, code, name }`" is, in `believedPin`, a pin whose `code` or `name` is not a human label, whose `id` is present and not in the team-id grammar, or that has no `id` off the bundled competition; a key other than those three is ignored, not a reason to drop the pin. `AGENTS.md` states it that way.

**The competition is decided ONCE, at the edge, and then travels as a value.** `resolveCompetition(explicit, env, saved)` is called in exactly two places, and core reads no environment and no file: the CLI's option resolution (`packages/cli/src/config.ts`: `--competition`, `CLAUDINHO_COMPETITION` and the saved choice → `cfg.selection`, and `cfg.competition`, its slug, `''` when refused or nothing is chosen) and the MCP server's request edge (`choiceOf`/`selectionOf` in `packages/mcp/src/tools.ts`: the tool's `competition` argument, the server's `CLAUDINHO_COMPETITION` and the saved choice, once per request, before `resolveAdapter` builds or reuses an adapter, which stays keyed by source and competition, with one throttle window per source shared by every adapter of it still alive, kept or evicted while a request holds it (weak refs, never what keeps one alive; one non-reentrant broadcast per throttle), and a bound on the kept adapters, `KEPT_ADAPTERS_MAX`, least recently used evicted). The SAVED CHOICE is the user's config file (`config.json`: `{ version: 1, competition, team? }`, at `configPath(env, platform, home)`: `$XDG_CONFIG_HOME/claudinho/`, else `%APPDATA%\claudinho\` on Windows, else `~/.config/claudinho/`), written only by `claudinho follow` (atomic, 0600 enforced, never through a link) and read at each edge, once per invocation or request, through core's one no-follow bounded reader (`readUserConfig`): a file that is absent, a symlink, unreadable, malformed, of another version or naming no competition the resolver's grammar accepts is NO saved choice (never a guess), with the reason kept for `follow` to print; a pin that is not exactly `{ id?, code, name }` (the team-id grammar, human labels) is dropped and the competition kept. The FIRST present source decides (flag, then environment, then the saved choice); an empty string is absent; with none, `{ kind: 'none' }`: nothing is chosen, and the finished World Cup is not a default anyone falls into (`DEFAULT_COMPETITION`/`BUNDLE_COMPETITION` name the bundle's slug for `bundleApplies`, never a selection). With none, nothing below the edge runs: every competition-answering CLI command throws ONE input error (`selectionRefusal`: "No competition chosen. Pick one: `claudinho follow premier-league` …", exit 1; on `--json` the object `{ competition: null, noCompetition: true }` on stdout first), with no request and no cache read; `team`, `follow`, `init` (which then says how to choose), `star` and `--version` need none; the statusline prints `⚽ claudinho follow` with no cache read and no refresher, the hook prints nothing, `vibe` its line with no segment, `_refresh` returns; every competition-answering MCP tool answers the `noCompetition` verdict (`selectionVerdict`, the first replacing verdict) before any adapter is built: its text the one sentence, its data the tool's own empty healthy shape (a `refusal` builder beside each answer, `said`) plus `competition: null` and `noCompetition: true`; `list_competitions` answers `current: null`, `standings://` the sentence, `get_team` as ever. Each value is an alias (`premier-league`), a slug in the table (`eng.1`), for a value without a dot a row's name, other name or short form (`Premier League`, `epl`: case, accents, the space, `-` and `_` ignored, 64 units at most; never a prefix), or a raw ESPN slug the table does not hold (`^[a-z0-9_]+(\.[a-z0-9_]+)+$`, 64 units at most: real slugs carry underscores, `esp.copa_del_rey`; `experimental`, named by its slug; one grammar, `competitionValue`, asked by the resolver and the config reader alike; `follow` saves the slug, never the typed form); anything else is REFUSED (`selectionRefusal`: the value, the nearest aliases when there are any, at most two by `suggestCompetitions`'s written steps, then the aliases; the CLI wraps it at the terminal's width, MCP never), never a request, even when a lower source is valid: every competition-reading CLI command (`init` and `star` read none) throws an input error before any request, an MCP tool a tool error before any adapter is built; the ambient commands contain it (`prompt` prints `⚽ —`, the hook nothing, `vibe` its line without a segment; no cache read, no refresher). `selectedCompetition` is the one constructor of a selected value. **The pin** (`follow <alias> --team <name>`, resolved exactly as `next <name>` resolves it: the bundle's nations offline by code, `{ code, name }`; a club through `next`'s own reads, `{ id, code, name }`; ambiguous or unknown writes nothing) belongs to its competition, not to the source that chose it: it applies whenever the selected competition IS the file's (an alias and its slug are one), whether the file, the flag or the environment chose it, and never under another competition (core `pinUnder`, asked by `cfg.pin` and MCP `choiceOf(...).pin`); a `follow` of another competition without `--team` drops it, and `follow --team` cannot pin while its competition is between editions (`next` answers the verdict before it resolves a club). The team-taking commands take, in this order, the argument, `CLAUDINHO_TEAM`, the pin (CLI `teamAsked`; MCP `get_next_fixture` the same, its `nextAsked`, the server's `CLAUDINHO_TEAM` read once at the request's edge, a tool error when there is none of the three); the pin answers as a RESOLVED team through `nextFixtureForPin` (built by `sealTeam` with the competition's written kind; its fixture by `isTeam`, the id deciding; by code for an id-less pin, which the reader believes on the bundled competition alone, its nations carrying no id; no roster read). A pin's labels are bounded as a team's (`TEAM_CODE_COLUMNS`, `MAX_LABEL_COLUMNS`: the writer copies a resolved team), its code upper-cased as a feed's is. A relative `XDG_*` value is invalid and ignored by both path rules (the base-directory specification). The ambient surfaces prefer, never filter: ONE function, `pickAmbientMatch(matches, pick)` (the statusline's live line, syncing matchup and countdown, the hook's list, `vibe`'s segment), puts the picked team's matches first and keeps the others (the statusline shows the picked live match and counts the rest, `+N`), the pick being `CLAUDINHO_TEAM`'s code, else the pin when `CLAUDINHO_TEAM` is absent (one the hot path cannot read, a name off the bundle, is still the override: no preference, never the pin). An adapter injected through a test seam must serve the selection's competition: one for another is refused before any read, as an input error (CLI `adapterFor`) or a tool error (MCP `resolveAdapter`), so a body is never one competition's under another's name. Below the edge the adapter STATES the competition it serves (`ProviderAdapter.competition`, required) and every function asks the adapter or the config — never the environment, never the config file, and never a default argument (the cache's path functions take a competition always). `packages/core/test/selection-identity.test.ts` fails on a call anywhere else, on one with no argument, or on an edge that does not hand in its flag, the environment and the saved choice.
